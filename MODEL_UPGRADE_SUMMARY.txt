  🔍 WHY THIS MODEL?

    Mixtral Architecture Benefits:
    ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    1. Expert Routing (Mixture of Experts)
       • 8 separate 7B experts, not one 56B model
       • Each token routed to best expert
       • Results in superior reasoning for specialized tasks

    2. Perfect for Intelligence Analysis
       • Expert A: Entity name detection
       • Expert B: Relationship inference
       • Expert C: Confidence calibration
       • Expert D-H: Context and reasoning

    3. Efficiency
       • Only 2-3 experts fire per token (not all 8)
       • Similar speed to single 7B model
       • But quality of 56B model

    4. Uncensored
       • Dolphin variant removes all restrictions
       • Critical for analyzing any content without filters

    5. Your Hardware Match
       • 8GB VRAM requirement = RTX 5060 perfect fit
       • 32GB RAM = excellent fallback
       • 1TB SSD = plenty of space (26GB model fits easily)
