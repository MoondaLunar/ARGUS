❓ FAQ

  Q: Will this work with my hardware?
  A: Yes! Dolphin-Mixtral-8x7b is optimized for 8GB VRAM.
     Your RTX 5060 is perfect. 32GB RAM is excellent.

  Q: Can I keep dolphin-llama3?
  A: Yes! Both models can coexist. Switch via:
     ollama_model="dolphin-llama3" in code

  Q: How long does download take?
  A: 5-20 minutes depending on internet speed (26GB).

  Q: How much disk space do I need?
  A: ~30GB free (26GB model + ~4GB temp space).

  Q: What if download fails?
  A: Check internet connection, then retry:
     ollama pull dolphin-mixtral:8x7b

  Q: Is it really uncensored?
  A: Yes. Dolphin variant removes all restrictions.
     Critical for intelligence analysis.

  Q: Can I use CPU-only instead?
  A: Yes, but inference would be 50-100x slower.
     GPU is recommended (takes ~5 sec vs ~5 min per entity pair).

  Q: How do I monitor resource usage?
  A: Task Manager → Performance → GPU tab
     Shows VRAM usage in real-time during crawl.
